Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how Fear Army LTD handles information when you use Dive Bar Finder or visit divebarfinder.info.

SupportPrivacy PolicyTerms of Use

Who we are

Dive Bar Finder is operated by Fear Army LTD. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@insanerabbit.com.

Information the app handles

Location

If you grant permission, the app uses your precise location to find nearby venues and calculate distance. Search coordinates are sent through our Cloudflare service to Google Places to return results. You can instead search using a city or postcode.

When you choose to check in, the app stores the venue location, your check-in coordinates, GPS accuracy, distance from the venue and check-in time in your synchronized collection. This is used to verify that you are physically near the venue. Location used only for an ordinary nearby search is not added to your collection.

Account information

If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier and, where Apple provides them, your name and email address. If you choose Hide My Email, we receive Apple’s relay address. We use this information to create and secure your account and synchronize your collection.

Collection and community contributions

We store visited venues, check-in information, collection statistics, achievements, venue approvals, venue flags, and related timestamps. Venue approvals and aggregate community signals may affect results shown to other users, but we do not display your name, email address or precise check-in coordinates publicly.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and payment information. The app receives product, transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock Premium and restore purchases. We do not receive your full card details.

Local app storage

The app stores settings, favourites, cached search results, venue details, collection snapshots and a secure session token on your device. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your Apple settings.

Website analytics

This website creates random anonymous browser and session identifiers and records page paths, page titles, broad referrer information and selected button clicks. The analytics code blocks form fields, search text, full URLs and common personal-data fields from event properties. Hosting and network providers may still process technical information such as IP addresses in ordinary server logs.

How we use information

  • Provide venue search, directions, filtering and location-based features.
  • Create accounts and synchronize collections and achievements.
  • Verify check-ins and reduce fraudulent or remote check-ins.
  • Moderate venue classifications using community approvals and flags.
  • Provide Premium entitlements and purchase restoration.
  • Measure reliability and improve the app and website.
  • Protect users, investigate misuse and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and platform services.
  • Cloudflare hosts our application service and account database.
  • Google Places supplies venue search results, details, ratings and photos.
  • Vercel hosts this website.

These providers may process information in countries outside your own and apply their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Account and collection information is retained while your account remains active. You can delete individual visited venues in the app. You can delete the entire account from Settings while signed in; this deletes the account, sessions, synchronized collection, achievements, approvals and flags from our active account database. Local cached data is also cleared. Infrastructure backups and security logs may persist for a limited period before routine deletion.

Website analytics are retained only as long as reasonably needed for product measurement and security. You can clear the site’s anonymous identifier through your browser’s site-data controls.

Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke location access, use manual city search, sign out, remove collection entries, delete your account and manage subscriptions through Apple. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal information. Contact us to make a request.

Children

Dive Bar Finder is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is rated 17+ on the App Store. It is not directed to children.

Security

We use HTTPS, hashed session tokens, Apple identity-token verification and access controls designed to protect account data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app, website or service providers change. The effective date above shows the latest revision. Material changes will be presented through an appropriate channel.
