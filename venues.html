For bars and venues

Your place.
Your details.

If your bar appears in Dive Bar Finder or one of our guides, we want the description and practical details to be accurate.

Corrections are free. We do not sell rankings or charge venues to appear higher in editorial guides.

Check your listing

Tell us what
needs changing.

Email support@insanerabbit.com with the venue name, city, official website and the detail that needs correcting. Useful updates include closure, relocation, opening hours, accessibility, age rules and a changed music programme.

For ownership-sensitive changes, write from an official venue address or include a link where the change is published. We review the evidence before updating editorial copy.
