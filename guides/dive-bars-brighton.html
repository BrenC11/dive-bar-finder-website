Brighton's compact centre layers LGBTQ+ nightlife, tiny gig rooms, old pubs and seaside weekend traffic into a few dense streets. A useful search starts with the kind of room you want, then narrows the city to a compact area where recent evidence and a realistic journey home line up.

What “dive bar” means in Brighton

The phrase “dive bar” does not map perfectly onto every local scene. In Brighton, useful search language includes alternative pub, punk gig, LGBTQ+ bar. Treat those as clues to a venue's purpose, then check current photos, programme details and reviews before travelling.
