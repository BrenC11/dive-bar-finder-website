What “dive bar” means in Mexico City

The phrase “dive bar” does not map perfectly onto every local scene. In Mexico City, useful search language includes bar de rock, foro, cantina. Treat those as clues to a venue's purpose, then check current photos, programme details and reviews before travelling.
