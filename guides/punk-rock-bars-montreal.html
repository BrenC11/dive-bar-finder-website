Montréal punk and rock bars: quick answers

Where can I find punk bars in Montréal?

Start with Les Foufounes Électriques and Turbo Haüs, then check Barfly, Piranha Bar and the current live-music programme around Saint-Denis and Saint-Laurent.

Where can I hear metal in Montréal?

Piranha Bar and Foufounes Électriques regularly host heavy music, but both are programme-led. Check the exact event before travelling.

Are Montréal punk bars open without a show?

Some operate as bars outside show times, while others change format around the ticketed room. Check current opening hours and whether the event affects entry.
