9 punk, metal and goth places in Berlin worth checking now.

Permanent bars and event-led club nights are different searches. This guide separates them, gives you current starting points and shows what to verify before setting off.

How to build the right Berlin shortlist

  1. Choose bar or event first. A Kneipe is useful on an ordinary night; a goth or punk event may be the reason to cross the city.
  2. Use the matching app filters. Combine Punk, Metal, Gothic and Unique or alternative according to the scene you want.
  3. Search at Kiez level. Kreuzberg and Friedrichshain are useful starting points, but they are not interchangeable.
  4. Verify today’s information. Check programme, cash or card policy, smoking conditions, age rules and last entry.
  5. Plan the return. Weekend transport differs from weekday service, so check BVG before settling on a distant option.

Berlin alternative nightlife: quick answers

Where can I find punk bars in Berlin?

Kreuzberg is the strongest starting point. SO36, Clash and Franken Bar are current options, but check the event and opening details before travelling.

What are the best metal bars in Berlin?

Paule’s Metal Eck and Rockcafé Halford are established metal bars in Friedrichshain. Berlin metal listings can help with one-off shows and smaller rooms.

Does Berlin have goth clubs?

Yes, but much of the scene is event-led. Dunckerclub runs Dark Monday, while Cassiopeia and independent promoters host goth, darkwave, EBM and industrial nights on selected dates.
