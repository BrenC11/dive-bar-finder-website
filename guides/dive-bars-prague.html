What “dive bar” means in Prague

The phrase “dive bar” does not map perfectly onto every local scene. In Prague, useful search language includes rockový bar, hospoda, hudební klub. Treat those as clues to a venue's purpose, then check current photos, programme details and reviews before travelling.
